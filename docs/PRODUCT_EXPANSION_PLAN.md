# Modus product expansion — owner direction, 28 October 2026

## Value

Your whole health history, from childhood to today, in one trustworthy timeline you can check every day.

The useful outcomes are collecting scattered reports, finding an exact fact and its original document, tracing events across facilities and years, and seeing new or unreviewed material at a glance. A polished three-metric screen does not establish those outcomes. Current extraction remains three lab metrics; broader records and native desktop delivery are requested work, not demonstrated capabilities.

## Required functions and acceptance

| Owner value | Function | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collect years of records | Folder/ZIP selection, document classification, locally retained originals, per-document review | Mixed Korean/English document fixtures import without silently dropping unsupported facts; every failure remains actionable. |
| Trust each fact | Verbatim label/value/unit, page/region, extractor and human review provenance | Each accepted field opens the original source region; unknown fields remain unknown; mismatches require review. |
| Trace a life | Visits, labs, vitals/growth, immunizations, recorded diagnoses/procedures/prescriptions and document-only reports on one timeline | Original occurred and system availability dates remain separate; facility/type/date filters and local profile ages work. |
| Glance daily | Today with newly available records, changed facts and pending review | Items derive from actual last-view/review state. Printed future dates remain attributed record statements. |
| Ask and carry records | Cited answers, scoped history, visit packet export | Every sentence has verified typed fact support; unsupported requests abstain; exported dates/provenance match the chosen view. |

Reference ranges and diagnoses may be retained as provider-written statements only. No generated diagnosis, risk score, treatment recommendation, genetics interpretation or inference that missing records mean missed care. Unit normalization requires a verified analyte-specific conversion contract and retained original value/unit, never a generic mg/dL conversion. Confidence cannot authorize an automatic commit or stand in for a measured error rate.
