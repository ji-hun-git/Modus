# What the Modus prompts actually delivered

This audit answers the owner's question: “Where did you implement the things in my prompts?” It separates the running competition entry from the broader local research application. A file existing in the research tree does not mean the feature is available in the app on port 8011.

The 28 October master prompt explicitly requested a current-health service and a light, off-white/deep-green interface. The subsequent 29 October contest instructions narrowed active behavior to record integrity: no generated diagnosis, interpretation, risk scores or advice. That narrowing explains why medical interpretation is parked. It does **not** justify leaving the requested visual design out of the active app.

## The correction in this design pass

The active interface now follows the supplied master design system. Home uses a composed history/evidence workspace instead of three equal oversized number cards. Observation points use real dates and exact source values; incompatible units stay separate, unknown dates stay explicit and no smooth invented trajectory or health verdict is drawn. Unsafe numeric conversions are omitted from charts while their exact records remain available. The evidence stack opens the original source context. One Ask, one Add records action and Home/Records/More remain the main structure. “Since 2018” comes from the example's actual earliest dated record, not an invented biography.

The comparison/review/evidence flows retain the existing human gate, both clocks, conflicts and original wording. No new clinical behavior is introduced under cover of a visual change. Held-out sets are closed and excluded from this work.

The complete lifelong-health product is **not fulfilled**. The immediate deliverable is the requested visual and interaction design in the app the owner actually uses, together with an honest map of implemented, parked and missing work.

## Next work, in order

1. Finish saved-record corrections: a reviewed amendment must preserve the old record, who changed it, why and when. Replay, conflicts, charts and briefs must respect the amendment's availability date. This closes the largest gap in the promise that an owner can correct their history.
2. Test the complete service with a larger authored history spanning all eight record kinds. Improve finding and grouping records before adding panels to Home. Review terminology and ambiguous extraction with a human; the closed held-outs stay closed.
3. Before real personal records: owner authorization, explicit cloud disclosure, encryption, deletion/recovery and a documented privacy threat review. Automatic masking must be measured; it cannot be promised from a projection filter alone.
4. Calibrate the evaluation jury against human labels and qualify representative extraction layouts. SAM crops and hosted Guard need working evidence before they are advertised. Clinical interpretation remains outside the contest entry; desktop and device integrations follow a reliable web service.
