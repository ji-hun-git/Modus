# Modus review readiness — 28 October 2026

Modus now demonstrates a useful factual workflow: upload supported records, review exact entries, inspect their sources and disagreements, replay what was available in Modus, and prepare a reviewed brief. The implementation is materially ahead of the older review. Consumer usefulness, broad model quality, production privacy and contest eligibility remain unproven. A numerical product score would imply a grading method we have not established.

This is a read-only code/document audit plus this report. No model request, Git mutation, credential read, or held-out access occurred. Private evaluation directories were not opened, enumerated, hashed, statted or checked for existence. Counts below come from named published project evidence, not a fresh private-suite inventory. The integrated milestone adds browser verification and a separately recorded public-source update.
